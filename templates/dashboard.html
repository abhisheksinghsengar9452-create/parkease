IoT Hardware & Gateway Integration

ParkEase connects to cameras, barriers, and ultrasonic slot sensors via standard MQTT telemetry:

MQTT Broker: mqtt://localhost:1883
Topic Structure: parkease/{device_type}/{device_code}/telemetry
Supported Formats: JSON payload with status, event, and timestamp

Registered hardware devices automatically transition to ONLINE when events are processed. Simulated hardware drivers are available in devices/simulator.py.
